17 / What This Exploration Taught Us

Sometimes the smallest problem reveals the bigger experience.

Communication isn't always verbal. Gestures, icons, directions and confirmations can become part of the interaction.
Simplicity requires decisions. Reducing the product to two primary actions created more clarity than adding features.
Offline can be an experience decision. For travel, availability can become part of the experience strategy.
Knowing what not to build is part of designing. Scope is not only a development constraint. It is a product decision.

Maybe travel communication isn't about translating everything.

Maybe it's about making intent easier to understand.
